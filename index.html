Simple Process

Food rescued in three steps

From surplus to served in minutes. Our platform handles matching, priority windows, and pickup coordination automatically.

📦

Donor Lists Food

Add food details, quantity, expiry time, dietary flags, and pickup location. The 30-minute NGO priority window activates instantly.

🏢

NGOs Get First Access

Registered NGOs have exclusive access for 30 minutes. After that, orphanages can also browse and claim unclaimed listings.

🤝

Secure Pickup via OTP

Donor approves the claim and issues a 6-digit pickup code. Claimer confirms arrival — food status updates to Picked in real-time.
